Why we are writing

A lake three centuries old

Vadale Talao, also called Ballaleshwar Talao, was built in the Peshwa era by Balajipant Bapat, beside the Ballaleshwar temple. For close to three hundred years it has been the heart of Old Panvel: a place of faith, festivals and evening walks.

70,000 tiny helpers

The lake is covered by Salvinia, an invasive floating weed. In 2026 the Panvel Municipal Corporation, guided by ICAR's Directorate of Weed Research, released about 70,000 weevils that eat only this weed and harm nothing else. Satellite images show the weed has begun to recede.

Why manual cleaning would hurt

The weevils, their eggs and their larvae live inside the weed. Pulling the weed out by hand or machine takes them out with it, and the weed that is left grows back faster than the few surviving weevils can multiply. Biological control is slow, but it lasts. It needs to be left undisturbed.
